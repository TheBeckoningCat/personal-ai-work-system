# PAWS v1.0 release notes

This is the first public release of PAWS.

## What's in it

- A folder of plain Markdown files with three starter units: the executive unit with the Architect, the Hub with the Secretary, and one unit for a subject you choose.
- Setup is a real first session. The Architect shows what the Builder saved, asks only what's missing, and lets you skip the extras. Anything you mention along the way lands in the Daily Note.
- [Boundaries](../Boundaries.md) is one page in your words saying what helpers may do without asking, what needs a yes and what's off limits. Setup asks about it and you can change it any time.
- Each persona has one profile with its job and its personality together, and one Desk page in its office.
- Begin Session and End Session are six steps each, on [Start Here](../Executive%20Unit/00%20Start%20Here/Start%20Here.md) and [End Session](../Executive%20Unit/00%20Start%20Here/End%20Session.md).
- The Daily Note is the one capture point. The Hub keeps the calendar of record and the project pointers.
- `Setup/Tools/` has a setup helper and a checker with a release mode and a workspace mode.
- `LICENSE.md` and `NOTICE.md` sit at the top of every download.

## Limits

Markdown records don't install timers, connectors or notifications. What a host can really read and save has to be checked in that host. Live runs with every AI host and reader haven't been done.
