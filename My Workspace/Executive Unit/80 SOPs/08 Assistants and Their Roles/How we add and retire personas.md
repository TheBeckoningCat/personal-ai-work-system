# How we add and retire personas

The starter has three personas. Add one only when the owner asks.

## Add

1. Agree on a stable ID, a display name and any other names it answers to, the unit it works in, and what it may do.
2. Make its profile from the [Persona template](../../Templates/Persona.md) and its desk from the [Desk template](../../Templates/Desk.md). Let the owner read the profile before it's used.
3. Add its row to [Workspace Settings](../../../Workspace%20Settings.md) and the [Staff Directory](../../00%20Start%20Here/Section%201/Staff%20Directory.md). Check every path works, then mark it active.

Session Log rows only come from real sessions. A new local page can never widen [Boundaries](../../../Boundaries.md).

## Retire

The owner confirms which persona and what happens to its work. Finish or park its open jobs and hand over projects and mail, and get a reply saying they were received. Mark the Staff Directory row retired and take back any standing permission. Keep the profile, decisions and history, and update links. Don't erase anything.

## Rename

A new display name just changes the names in Workspace Settings, the Staff Directory and the profile. The [Personalization Map](../../../Package/Personalization%20Map.md) lists what changes. Moving folders is a separate job the owner looks over.
