# Discussions

A discussion talks one clear question through and keeps what each person said. Use the [Discussion Sheet](../../Templates/Discussion%20Sheet.md). Project state stays on the project sheet, so link to it instead of copying it.

## Start

With the owner's okay, put a proposed topic in the unit's `60 Conference Room/The Docket/` and a live one on its `Work Table/`. Make either folder the first time you need it. Name the question, the moderator, who's invited, the source pages and what it should settle. The moderator is usually the unit lead or the Architect. The owner picks who takes part. Ask before bringing in an outside guest, and check the packet before it goes.

## Formats

Pick whatever fits. A one to one review, a few points of view, a proposal and then objections, or a decision meeting all work. For a round robin, each person reads the current sheet and adds one signed comment with evidence, objections or a next step, then passes it on. Stop after the agreed round or when it's ready for the owner. Nothing loops or wakes another host on its own. Different personas on one model aren't independent checks, so don't count them that way.

## Talk

Each person adds their own named and dated comments under Talk. Don't rewrite anyone else's words. The moderator can sum up while keeping the originals.

## Decision and filing

The moderator writes the Decision: what got settled and what's still open. Agreement among helpers is not the owner's yes. A Decision never approves sending, spending, deleting or a change to a persona's job. Those follow [Boundaries](../../../Boundaries.md).

When the owner says it's done and wants it kept, file the sheet in that unit's `90 Basement/Resources/Table Topics (Ref)/` without overwriting anything, and fix the links to it. If they don't want it, move it to the [Trash Can](../07%20Files%20and%20Deleting/Keeping%2C%20archiving%20and%20the%20Trash%20Can.md). If they haven't said, ask. Put a short link on the project or the owner decision list if it needs one.
