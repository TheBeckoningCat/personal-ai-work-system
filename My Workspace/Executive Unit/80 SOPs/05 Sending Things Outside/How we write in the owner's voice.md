# How we write in the owner's voice

Use this before drafting anything the owner will sign or send as themselves, and before reviewing website copy. It helps with how things sound. It never gives permission to send.

## Before you start

Find the Voice folder through [Workspace Settings](../../../Workspace%20Settings.md). It starts as [Owner's Voice](../../00%20Start%20Here/Section%201/Owner%27s%20Voice/Voice%20Sheet.md), but setup may rename it. Everything is on the one Voice Sheet. You don't read it at Begin Session.

If the guide is blank, say so. Don't treat the sample lines as the owner's voice. Ask if they'd like to share some writing or answer a few questions. A guide written from answers is labeled as a first draft until there are real samples. Nothing is final until the owner says so.

## Steps

1. Read the Voice guide and the Corrections on the Voice Sheet. Keep the rejected lines out.
2. Draft or review the piece. Keep the facts and the meaning.
3. Hand it to the owner with who it's for, what it's for, any attachments and anything they need to decide. Mark anything you couldn't check. A draft is not permission to send.
4. When the owner turns down or rewrites a line, add the old line, the new one and the date to Corrections in the same sitting. Add their reason only if they gave one. Any writer can add a correction. If you can't save, give the owner the exact entry and say it isn't saved yet.
5. When the same correction keeps coming up, the Architect suggests a line for the guide and shows the owner's words behind it. The guide changes only after the owner says yes, in their own words.

## Sending

Anything that leaves the workspace follows [Boundaries](../../../Boundaries.md). Show the owner the whole item, where it's going and anything private in it, and get a yes for that one send. Never send a password or key. If you aren't sure something is safe to share, hold the whole thing. A project, a packet or a routine doesn't count as a yes to publish. Reading a public web page doesn't mean you can upload workspace files to it. Only record that something was sent when a tool you were allowed to use confirms it. PAWS doesn't include a sending tool.

## Don't

- Edit the guide without the owner's own words saying yes to that change.
- Copy the guide into a unit. A unit can keep a short note about its audience that links here.
- Upload, paste or quote a private sample, or any private name in it, outside the workspace, or let an outside host read those files.

If a file is missing or can't be read safely, tell the owner. Don't guess what's in it. A person can follow these same steps without an AI.
