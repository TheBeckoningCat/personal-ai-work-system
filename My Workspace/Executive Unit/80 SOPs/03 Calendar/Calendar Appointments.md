# Calendar Appointments

Every unit has one calendar page, `30 Calendar/Calendar Appointments.md`, with Requests, Approved, Past and Reminders. The Hub's Approved list is the calendar of record for the whole workspace. Other units keep Requests and pointers to the Hub's event IDs, never a second copy of the details. Undated work goes on a project, not the calendar.

## Record a date

1. Check the source and any existing event ID first so nothing gets added twice. A date or plan the owner said is enough to write it down. An invitation from someone else isn't a yes.
2. Under the calendar Requests duty, add it to the owning unit's Requests with an event ID, the date and start and end (or all day), the computer's zone, what it is, where it came from, what the owner said and its state. Write unknown for anything you don't know. Ask instead of guessing.
3. The Hub looks over Requests in every unit for clashes, length, travel, prep time and repeats, and reports anything that clashes or isn't clear. A date the owner hasn't accepted stays a Request.
4. When the owner said or accepted the date and nothing clashes, the Hub writes one entry under Approved on its calendar with `OK Hub` and where it came from, and points the Request to it. Don't ask again just to write it down.
5. Keep old values and cancellations. A changed date updates the same event after a fresh clash check. Move past entries to Past with their sources.

## Reminders

A reminder brings something back to the owner's attention. It doesn't promise work that day. Under the reminders duty, add owner given dates and real deadlines to the Reminders part of the owning unit's calendar page, with an ID, the date and zone, what it's for and where it came from. Never invent a date to clear a list. Say what's due when you start work, keep finished ones in Past and leave open ones showing.

## Repeats and outside calendars

Write down the agreed pattern, zone, exceptions and start and end. A pattern on a page doesn't set anything off. No outside calendar, sync or alert is installed. Sending an invitation or changing an outside account follows [Boundaries](../../../Boundaries.md).
