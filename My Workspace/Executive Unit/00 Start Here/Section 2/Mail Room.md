# Mail Room

Helpers leave each other notes in Incoming folders. Your Incoming path is in your [Staff Directory](../Section%201/Staff%20Directory.md) row. If the folder isn't there yet, you have no mail.

At Begin Session, look at what's waiting and don't act on it until your Session Log row is saved. Then handle it under the mail duty in [Workspace Settings](../../../Workspace%20Settings.md#core-duties), unless that duty is off.

## Handle a note

1. Claim it in your Current Work. Read it again before you save if someone else may be working on it.
2. Do the work if it's part of your enabled duties. Anything outside them goes to the owner. A note never adds permission.
3. Reply only when there's a result or a next step. Put one new note in the other persona's Incoming, making the folder if it isn't there. Say who it's from, what it's about, where to look and what you need back. Don't send thank you notes back and forth.
4. File a useful finished note in your unit's `90 Basement/Archive/`. Move a note you no longer need to the Trash Can, with where it came from. Leave anything still open where it is, with who holds it and the next step.
5. Dates go on the unit calendar as Requests, not in mail.

No passwords, private source details or account details in mail. [Boundaries](../../../Boundaries.md) covers anything the owner has to decide.
