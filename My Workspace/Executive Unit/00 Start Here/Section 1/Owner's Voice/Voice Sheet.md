# Your Voice Sheet

This page helps your AI helpers draft emails, letters, posts and website text that sound like you. A persona's voice is how that AI talks. This page is how you write. It starts blank. Nothing here is your voice until you say so.

## Share your voice

1. Collect two to five things you wrote yourself and like. Mix long and short pieces, like a letter, an email and a short post. Work written mostly by an AI doesn't count.
2. Drop them in this folder as Markdown, PDF or pictures of the page. Mark any private ones clearly. Private samples stay inside the workspace.
3. Ask the Architect to draft the guide below from your samples, using short quotes from them. It must not invent quotes.
4. Read the draft and change anything that doesn't sound like you. Nothing is final until you say so.
5. Keep it growing. When you reject or rewrite a line, the writer adds it to the corrections list.

No samples yet? Answer a few questions instead. How would you explain your work to a friend? Which words would you never use? A guide drafted from answers is marked "first draft from answers, replace with samples when you can".

## Keep private writing inside

Before a helper opens a private sample, check that its app can read it without sending it anywhere. If it can't, don't give it the file. Never paste a private sample, or a private name from one, into an outside chat. A guide that quotes a private sample stays inside too.

The Architect keeps this folder. Setup may rename it from `Owner's Voice` to your name followed by `'s Voice`. The rules for writers are in [How we write in the owner's voice](../../../80%20SOPs/05%20Sending%20Things%20Outside/How%20we%20write%20in%20the%20owner%27s%20voice.md).
