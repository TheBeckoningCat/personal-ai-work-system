# Session Log

One row per session. Add yours at Begin Session step 4 and mark it `out` at the end.

| Session ID | Persona ID / display name | Host label / access | Status | Started at / zone | Last update / zone | Current Work / proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Status is `in`, `out` or `interrupted`. Use a new ID like `hub-laptop-cli-20261102-01` and the computer's clock. Current Work names the job and its pages. Settle clashes before you save.

Edit only your own row, and resuming your own session keeps it. Never reset another `in` row because it looks old. Ask the owner. A guest who can't save hands the owner the row. Invited helpers have none.
