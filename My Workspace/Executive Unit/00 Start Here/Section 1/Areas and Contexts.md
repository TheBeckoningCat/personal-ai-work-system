# Areas and Contexts

Areas are the parts of life or work a project belongs to. Contexts are where or how a next step gets done. Both are labels to help find and review work. Neither one gives permission or sets a date, and an area doesn't need its own unit.

## Areas of Life

| Stable area | Display label | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| executive | Executive Unit | Shared design and rules |
| hub | Hub Unit | Commitments and administrative records |
| example | Example Unit | Set during setup |

The owner may add areas without adding units. Setup updates the labels and purposes after the owner agrees.

## Contexts

Optional labels on a next step, like `[Desk]`, `[Phone]` or `[WITH a named person]`. Use them only when they help. A label never sets up a meeting or sends a message. Keep them on the project sheet rather than in a second list.
