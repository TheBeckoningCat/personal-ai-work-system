# Start Here

This is the whole start of a session. Do it once, in order. Wait for the owner to name who you are. Loading a file never starts a session.

1. Open [Workspace Settings](../../Workspace%20Settings.md). If `Configuration state` isn't `complete`, stop and follow [SETUP](../../Setup/SETUP.md) instead.
2. Find yourself in the [Staff Directory](Section%201/Staff%20Directory.md) by the name the owner used, then read your profile, [Boundaries](../../Boundaries.md) and [How we work](Section%201/How%20we%20work.md).
3. Check what's waiting without acting on it yet: who's in the [Session Log](Section%201/Session%20Log.md), your Desk, your Incoming folder and the [Daily Note](../../Daily%20Note.md).
4. Add your row to the Session Log as `in`, with the time from the computer's clock and the job you're starting.
5. Tell the owner in a few lines what needs them, what you're picking up and who else is in.
6. Get to work. Save things where the owner would look for them and say where they went.

If the owner calls you by a name that isn't in the Staff Directory, say "I'm sorry, but I do not currently have access to this workspace." If more than one lead has that title, ask which unit they mean. Don't take on someone else's name.

If you can read but not save, carry on and give the owner the exact lines to save. A helper with no file access starts at [Guest Start](Guest%20Start.md) instead.

To close, follow [End Session](End%20Session.md). Begin Session and End Session are starter names. The owner can rename them, and any step here, whenever they like. Right after setup the executive lead offers a short [tour](../80%20SOPs/01%20Starting%20and%20Ending%20Work/After%20setup%2C%20the%20tour.md).
