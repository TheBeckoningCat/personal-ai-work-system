## Where the records start

Each unit has `01 Inbox` and `40 Potential Projects`, and a Reminders part on its `30 Calendar/Calendar Appointments.md` page. Project sheets have the three action sections. `Next Actions.md` at the top opens the project lists, and `Projects Base.md` holds the project pointers. The Daily Note has Must do and Needs a decision parts. Choosing new words doesn't move these.

Each of these places carries a quiet marker, so a later add-on can find it without guessing the word you chose. To change a word, follow [Terms and Markers](Package/Terms%20and%20Markers.md). The [Personalization Map](Package/Personalization%20Map.md) covers the other setup choices.
