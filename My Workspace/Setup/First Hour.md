# Your first hour

Setup already puts you in a working session, so this hour is about trying the loop that matters: do a little real work, End Session, then check that a fresh session picks it up. Nothing here needs an install or an account.

## 1. Do one small real thing, about fifteen minutes

Tell the persona you are with three to five things on your mind. It captures your words in the Daily Note and sorts out which are commitments, ideas, dates and open choices. Pick one manageable outcome. Say: "Help me plan this as a small project. Record my outcome and one useful next action."

Your outcome is approval for its Markdown records. Anything bigger follows [Boundaries](../Boundaries.md). Look for a project folder and sheet, the next action, and a link the Hub can find in Projects Base. Nothing should be sent outside. Avoid private data or costly commitments in a first try.

## 2. End Session, about ten minutes

Say: "Please end the session by following the executive unit's 00 Start Here/End Session.md." Then check the project sheet, What's next on the persona's Desk, the Work Log entry and the `out` row in the Session Log. A reply that says "saved" isn't enough. Open the files.

## 3. Resume, about ten minutes

Open a fresh chat, or another host with the same folder. Say: "You are the Architect. Please begin a session by reading Workspace Settings.md and following the executive unit's 00 Start Here/Start Here.md." Use another persona's name from the Staff Directory if you like. It should find the project and the pickup note without relying on chat memory, use a new session ID, and lead its report with what needs you and the next step.

## Next, if you want

Try a calendar Request, a handoff between units or a Guest review. An outside calendar is optional.

If you'd like to gather up loose commitments, ideas and notes, do it in small batches. Drop them in the Daily Note and ask the Secretary where each should go: a project, a reminder, a reference, Potential Projects or nowhere. Look over the suggestions before anything starts or gets thrown away, and keep the originals. Don't upload private archives in one go. You don't need a full inventory of your life to use PAWS.

Sharing your writing later through the [Voice Sheet](../Executive%20Unit/00%20Start%20Here/Section%201/Owner%27s%20Voice/Voice%20Sheet.md) is optional and isn't part of this hour.
