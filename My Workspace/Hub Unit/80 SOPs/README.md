# Hub procedures

The Hub's own steps. The shared procedures in the [SOP Index](../../Executive%20Unit/80%20SOPs/SOP%20Index.md) still apply, and they win if a page here disagrees.

<!-- paws-term:inbox -->
## Daily Note and Inbox

The [Daily Note](../../Daily%20Note.md) is the one capture point for the owner and every persona. Keep it readable. Leave each capture in the owner's words and add a short note on where it went. Leave anything unsorted under Capture. Don't swap their words for a summary or invent urgency. For a new day, file the old note in `Hub Unit/90 Basement/Archive/Daily Notes/` without overwriting, then start a fresh one from the [Daily Note template](../../Executive%20Unit/Templates/Daily%20Note.md). Don't change the date just because a host started.

Unit inboxes hold files and handoffs, and mail is separate. Sort captures the way [How we Capture](../../Executive%20Unit/80%20SOPs/02%20Projects/How%20we%20Capture.md) says. The Hub never starts a project, sets a date or throws away a source just to finish sorting.

## Calendar of record

After your Session Log row is saved, review every unit's calendar Requests and any due reminders. Check dates, zones, length, travel, prep time and repeats, and flag clashes or missing facts. When the owner said or accepted the date and there's no clash, write it under Approved on the [Hub calendar](../30%20Calendar/Calendar%20Appointments.md) with `OK Hub`, and point the Request to it. Keep old values and past entries. The full steps are in the [calendar procedure](../../Executive%20Unit/80%20SOPs/03%20Calendar/Calendar%20Appointments.md).

<!-- paws-term:projects-base -->
## Projects Base and decisions

Keep one row per project in the [Projects Base](../../Projects%20Base.md), pointing to the sheet. Keep [Needs the Owner's Decision](../../Needs%20the%20Owner%27s%20Decision.md) as the one list of open choices. Point to the source instead of copying private detail.

## Reviews

The owner picks how often to review and what to look at. Offer an evening look at unsorted captures, upcoming dates and open decisions only when they ask for it. Nothing here runs on a timer. A private journal is never read unless the owner asks, and then only the parts they choose. The [Daily Journal template](../../Executive%20Unit/Templates/Daily%20Journal.md) is there if they want one.

## Keeping the source

When you make a summary, a project, a reminder or a pointer, keep the original and link back to it. Keep nuance, doubts and specialist detail. If a batch is too big to handle well, do a smaller one and say so. Don't squash everything to fit.
